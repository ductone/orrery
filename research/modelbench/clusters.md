## Caveats

- E2 costs (list price x global effort multiplier) ignore per-model verbosity; their log sd is ~1.1, so frontier membership resting on E2 cost (gpt-6-luna, gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-oss-120b) is provisional.
- Effort deviations for unmeasured efforts default to the global effort curve (+-0.6 prior sd).
- Ceiling ignores `max`, which most routes do not expose.
- No speed axis: only FrontierSWE reports duration.
